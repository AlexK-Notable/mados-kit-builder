How to build

  1. Take a part. Drag it out of the list, or click it to stand it on the floor. The box above the list finds a part by name, number or size.
  2. Hold it over another part. A green shape shows where it will go, and green rings mark plugs it can join. If it cannot go there, the builder says why.
  3. Let go, and it snaps in. Carry on until it is built, then press Download for printing to see how many of each part to print and to get the files.

Why will two blocks not join? Plain blocks only have sockets, so two of them need something between: a twist block, a Mini Block Half C, or a joiner pushed or screwed into the opening in the middle of a face (the grub joiner is the simplest). A part only goes where its plugs meet sockets the right way round, and never through another part.

What does "generated" mean? The official range has fixed sizes. Parts marked generated are other sizes, made by this builder from the official plug and socket shapes. A generated block and twist block have been test-printed against official parts; the others have not.

Your build is kept in this browser as you go. Rows in the "To print" list can be clicked to find those parts. Parts follow the open MADOS system by MADOS 3D (CC0). This builder is an unofficial community project.

Make a part in another size

This copy of the builder can only use the parts in the list.

The part is built from the same plug and socket shapes as the official parts, at the size you choose. It then appears in the list like any other.

How tightly the plugs grip: 0 is the loosest, 9 the tightest. The author's advice is to print one first, then go lower if the joint is too hard to push in and higher if it is too weak.
