How to build

  • Drag a part from the list on the left into the scene (the box above the list finds a part by name or number). Hold it over another part: the green outline shows where it will go. Let go and it snaps in.
  • Let go over the empty floor to stand a part there.
  • While dragging, press R to spin the part and F to tip it over.
  • Drag a placed part to move it. Hold Shift to move everything joined to it as well.
  • Click a part to select it. Delete removes it, Ctrl+D makes a copy. Hinges and wrap covers show buttons to swing or fold them.
  • Drag the empty background to look around, scroll to zoom, right-drag to slide the view.
  • Download for printing gives you one file for each kind of part, with how many of each to print.

Plain blocks only have sockets, so two of them need something between: a twist block, a mini block half, or a grub joiner screwed into the threaded hole in the middle of a face. Parts follow the open MADOS system by MADOS 3D (CC0). A part only goes where its plugs meet sockets the right way round, and never through another part.
